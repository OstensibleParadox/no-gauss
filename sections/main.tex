\begin{abstract}
For a non-Gaussian i.i.d. product with a positive density, finite second
moment, and finite location and scale Fisher information, we classify
measure equivalence under arbitrary bounded invertible linear mixing.
The equivalence group consists of Hilbert--Schmidt perturbations of the
signed permutations preserving the coordinate law.
A weighted Fourier jet supplies the local coercivity behind this result.
When the density has zeros, the same Sobolev assumptions give an exact
criterion: Hilbert--Schmidt proximity and avoidance of the zero set by
every row of the operator and its inverse.  Boundary geometry simplifies
this classification:
centered half-line laws permit only permutations, and bounded laws permit
only signed permutations satisfying a scalar reflection criterion.
These support classifications require no Fisher information.
Block examples distinguish equivalence from positive Hellinger affinity
and exhibit both linear and quadratic boundary-loss thresholds.
\end{abstract}
