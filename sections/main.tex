\begin{abstract}
Feldman–Hájek theory gives an exact measure-class classification for Gaussian
measures, but what replaces it for a non-Gaussian product law under arbitrary
linear mixing? Kakutani’s criterion decides equivalence of infinite product
measures, but it breaks down under bounded invertible linear mixing. We give
one consolidated, complete answer and it is rigid.
\end{abstract}
