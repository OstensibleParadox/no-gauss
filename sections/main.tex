\begin{abstract}
Let \(\mu=f^{\otimes\N}\) be a centered, variance-one, non-Gaussian
product law on \(\R^{\N}\).  Under positivity of the density and finite
location and scale Fisher information, we classify all bounded invertible
linear maps on \(\ell^2\) that preserve the measure class of \(\mu\).
The criterion is exact: \(T_\#\mu\sim\mu\) precisely when
\(TQ^{-1}-I\) is Hilbert--Schmidt for an allowed signed coordinate
permutation \(Q\).  Outside this class the two measures are mutually
singular.  The analytical mechanism is a bounded rotational Fourier
feature whose first variation vanishes identically only for Gaussian
laws.  It yields dimension-free local Hellinger coercivity; a block-sum
compactness argument upgrades this to global coercivity on uniformly
well-conditioned finite-dimensional matrices.  Measurable row-series
realizations, likelihood martingales, and a rational-translation
zero--one law then turn the finite-dimensional estimates into an
infinite-dimensional dichotomy.  We conclude with precise consequences
for functional independent component analysis, prior-preserving
reparameterizations in Bayesian inverse problems, and linear flow
matching.
\end{abstract}
