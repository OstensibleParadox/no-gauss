\section{Finite-Variance Equivalence and Global Rigidity}
\label{sec:finite-variance-equivalence-and-global-rigidity}

Because the product sample space is \(\R^{\N}\), not \(\ell^2\), we first
realize bounded operators by compatible measurable row series, establish the
orbit dichotomy, and recover the full affinity from finite marginals.  We then
prove the finite-variance classification: Hilbert--Schmidt perturbations yield
convergent likelihood ratios, whereas nonsummable tail deviations produce a
Hellinger obstruction.  The classification finally supplies the compactness
input for global finite-dimensional coercivity.
