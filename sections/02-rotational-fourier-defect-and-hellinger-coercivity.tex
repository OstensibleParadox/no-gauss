\section{Rotational Fourier Defect and Hellinger Coercivity}
\label{sec:rotational-fourier-defect-and-hellinger-coercivity}

Non-Gaussianity is first detected by a bounded, logarithm-free rotational
Fourier defect: if it vanishes at every frequency pair, a global linear ODE
forces the coordinate law to be Gaussian
\cite{kac1939characterization,kagan1973characterization}.  The matrix Fisher
operator then identifies the infinitesimal geometry, and finite real Fourier
sums turn a nonzero defect into dimension-free dual tests.  This yields local
Frobenius coercivity.  We also state the global form modulo the exact linear
stabilizer; its proof follows the infinite-dimensional classification.
