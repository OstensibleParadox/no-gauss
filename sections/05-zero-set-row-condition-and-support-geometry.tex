\section{Density Zeros and Support Rigidity}
\label{sec:zero-set-row-condition-and-support-geometry}

When the density has zeros, Hilbert--Schmidt proximity controls Hellinger
accumulation but not absolute continuity.  The missing condition is whether
the rows of \(T\) and \(T^{-1}\) land almost surely in \(\{f>0\}\).  We prove
this criterion and then show that bounded or one-sided support forces
permutation rigidity.  Each result states its own hypotheses.
