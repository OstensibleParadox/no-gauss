ZERO-SET ROW CONDITION AND SUPPORT GEOMETRY: CONSISTENCY REVIEW

Reviewed source
sections/parts/zero-set-and-support-classification.tex

Scope
The coordinate law is centered with variance one.  The density-zero
operator theorem assumes a non-Gaussian density f whose zero extension
satisfies sqrt(f) in W^{1,2}(R) and x(sqrt(f))' in L2(R); it does not assume
that f is positive almost everywhere.  The scale condition is omitted only
in the stated orthogonal case.  The bounded-support and half-line
classification is a separate result and does not require a density or
Fisher information.

Canonical definitions and macro-expansion checks
- U means exactly {x:f(x)>0}; it is not the closed support of the law.
- The zero-set row condition means that every indicated row series lands in
  U almost surely.  The forward condition concerns T; the inverse condition
  concerns T^{-1}.  Expanding the term at every occurrence gives the
  displayed row probabilities in the theorem.
- P is the exact linear stabilizer of the finite-variance coordinate law:
  coordinate permutations with precisely those signs that preserve rho.
  It is distinct from P^{+/-}, the full signed-permutation group used for
  symmetric stable coordinates.
- Phi_T is the measurable row-series realization constructed in Section 3,
  and nu_T=(Phi_T)_#mu throughout.
- G_rho={T in GL(H):nu_T is equivalent to mu}.  Gamma is reserved for a
  countable operator group in the measurable-realization argument.
- Tail_n(M) is the sum of the forward and reverse finite-marginal
  likelihood-ratio tails above M.  It is not the projected Hellinger energy.

Logical dependency check
1. Hilbert--Schmidt proximity gives finite-coordinate approximants whose
   image laws converge in Hellinger distance, hence in total variation.
2. On U^N each approximant is absolutely continuous relative to mu.  Total
   variation convergence transfers this restricted absolute continuity to
   nu_T.
3. The complement of U^N carries the singular part, so the forward
   zero-set row condition is exactly the remaining obstruction to
   nu_T << mu once Hilbert--Schmidt proximity is known.
4. Applying the same statement to T^{-1}, together with the compatible
   measurable inverse, gives equivalence and the two-sided criterion.
5. For bounded and half-line supports, conditional expectations turn the
   boundary restriction into row constraints that force monomial, then
   permutation, operators as stated.
6. The block examples separate factorwise absolute continuity from
   accumulation of projected Hellinger energy; they do not weaken the
   zero-set row condition.

Notation boundary
The operator diag extracts the diagonal part of a matrix.  Diagonal matrices
constructed from a sequence are described in words, so this notation has a
single expansion.  Gamma shape and scale are
r_Gamma and theta_Gamma, avoiding the stable exponent alpha.  The exact
finite-marginal criterion uses ell_n for the density ratio and Tail_n(M) for
its two-sided uniform-integrability tail.
