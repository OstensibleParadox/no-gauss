Method
Each phrase was enclosed in quotation marks and submitted to Google Scholar.
The successful results were read in the Codex in-app browser through its public
Scholar page, using accessibility text and, when text was incomplete, its DOM
snapshot. Search-result titles and context were inspected; hit counts were not
used as reliable bibliometric evidence. Hyphenation and punctuation may be
normalized by Scholar even for quoted queries.
No result means only that this exact query displayed no articles at that time.
It does not establish novelty, uniqueness, nonexistence of synonyms, or priority.
A hit is not automatically a harmful collision. Standard usage, unrelated
disciplinary usage and a nearby competing technical meaning are distinguished.
The collision table is a review record, not a claim that all papers returned by
Scholar were read in full.

Actionable nearby collision
'Projected likelihood' is already used for projection of likelihood ratios onto
a space of inference functions in semiparametric models:
D. L. McLeish and C. G. Small, Biometrika 79(1), 93-102 (1992).
Primary publisher DOI: https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/79.1.93
Publisher abstract inspected through official Oxford Academic search:
https://academic.oup.com/biomet/article-pdf/79/1/93/803536/79-1-93.pdf
The abstract describes projection onto an inference-function space and a
first-order relation to quasi-likelihood.
A separate current use is a Gaussian-process training objective built from
lower-dimensional data projections:
E. Cazelles and F. Tobar, https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16332
The primary arXiv abstract was inspected.
These semantic uses differ from this manuscript's finite-coordinate
Radon-Nikodym derivatives. The manuscript therefore uses the descriptive name
'finite-marginal likelihood ratio'. Its exact Scholar query displayed no articles;
this is not a claim that the mathematical construction is new.
No bibliography entry is required merely to justify an editorial avoidance of
a competing name. These sources document the naming decision in this audit.
